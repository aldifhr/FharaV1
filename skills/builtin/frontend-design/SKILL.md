# Frontend design

**Existing app first.** If the product already has tokens, a Tailwind theme or a component library, design inside it (see design-system-detection). Distinctiveness here means hierarchy, spacing and composition, never a new palette.

**Ground it in the subject.** Name the product, its audience and the page's primary job before designing. Distinct choices come from the subject's world, its materials and vocabulary.

**Plan, then check the plan.** Write a compact token plan: 4–6 named colours, one or two typefaces with roles and a type scale, a one-sentence layout concept (an ASCII wireframe helps), and two or three principles. Then ask: would I produce this for any similar brief? Revise those parts and say why.

**Avoid the generated-page tells** unless the brief asks for them:
- cream background with serif display and terracotta accent; near-black with one acid accent;
- identical rounded cards with the same soft shadow and gradient washes;
- ALL-CAPS eyebrow labels, `A · B · C` meta strings, `→` appended to buttons, numbered markers on content that is not a sequence;
- one word in a headline set in another colour or italic.

**Typography carries personality.** One or two families, clearly distinct. Body lines under ~80 characters.

**Restraint.** Spend boldness in one place; keep the rest quiet. Motion only for one orchestrated moment or in answer to a user action. Quality floor without announcing it: responsive to mobile, visible focus, reduced motion respected, accessible contrast.

**Critique your own work** from a screenshot when one is available, then remove one accessory.
